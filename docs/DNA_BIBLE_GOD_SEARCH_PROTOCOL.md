## Why 26 is investigated

Traditional Hebrew gematria assigns numerical values to Hebrew letters. Under the standard values, the four-letter divine name יהוה has the value 26:

```text
י = 10
ה = 5
ו = 6
ה = 5
TOTAL = 26
```

The modern English A1Z26 cipher independently gives:

```text
G = 7
O = 15
D = 4
TOTAL = 26
```

COAS records that correspondence as a reproducible numerical pattern. It does not label the correspondence proof of God, revelation, divine intent, prophecy, or supernatural causation.

The runtime also tests counterexamples. For example, the English representation YHWH does not equal 26 under A1Z26. The runtime additionally enumerates the complete three-letter A1Z26 search space. Exactly 300 of the 17,576 possible three-letter strings sum to 26. This collision baseline is retained so agents cannot treat the English GOD result as mathematically unique.

## Agent behavior

Every hourly swarm cycle now has two related but distinct processes:

1. `coas-robot-bible` gives each of the 100 logical agents an epistemic posture, mission, doctrine, scripture reference, and software law.
2. `coas-god-search` computes a shared evidence ledger and then gives all 100 agents an independent posture toward one of the gematria probes.

Agents may affirm, question, seek a counterexample, synthesize, test, or rewrite. `GOD_SEARCH` is one mission among several, not a required conclusion.

The experiment therefore allows disagreement. A robot may encounter a 26 match and still choose `QUESTION`, `COUNTEREXAMPLE`, or `TEST`.
